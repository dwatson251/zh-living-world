Living World city map packs
===========================

Each .lwcity file is the real-world part of a city map (height, surface, buildings, trees, roads, spawn lots) for one of the
server's map codenames: <city>-<size> for london, newyork, paris, dubai and small, medium, large. The server and every client build the actual Zero Hour map from the
pack and their own game files the first time it is needed (Documents\Command and Conquer Generals Zero Hour Data\Maps\
Living World <City> <Size>\). No game files are in these packs.

Scale: 1 terrain cell = 2 m. Sizes, all around the same centre: small 710 cells = 1.4 km (the largest retail Zero Hour map size),
medium 2400 cells = 4.8 km, large 5000 cells = 10 km.

Made with tools/citygen/citygen.py from open data:

    https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Extracts from Geofabrik (download.geofabrik.de).
  - Elevation: Copernicus GLO-30 Digital Elevation Model, (c) DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and (c) Airbus Defence and Space GmbH
    2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.

The packs are derived databases of OpenStreetMap data and are made available under the ODbL 1.0 as well.
